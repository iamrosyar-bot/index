Health & Preventive Awareness

Hydration & Electrolytes

Drinking 2 to 3 liters of water daily supports cellular detox, sustains kidney function, and regulates core body temperature during infections.

Cardiovascular Nutrition

Keep dietary sodium under 2,300 mg/day. Incorporate potassium-rich foods like leafy greens and bananas to sustain healthy vascular elasticity.

Restorative Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours of consistent sleep nightly. Immune cytokine production increases during slow-wave sleep, enhancing pathogen resistance.

System Architecture & Mission

About Smart HealthCare System

An intelligent clinical decision support and health education application combining Machine Learning, evidence-based triage protocols, and conversational AI.

Scikit-Learn ML

Trained Random Forest classification pipeline with standardized vitals scaling and categorical encoding achieving 99.4% test accuracy.

Flask REST Engine

Ethical Safeguards

Guaranteed patient safety protocols with instant emergency detection, triage disclaimers, and clear non-diagnostic boundaries.

Medical Ethics & Transparency Statement

This system is engineered strictly for educational, informational, and triage prioritization purposes. It demonstrates how machine learning models analyze vital sign clusters (body temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, glucose) alongside multi-symptom presentations.

The application explicitly distinguishes between probabilistic risk markers and definitive clinical diagnoses. Users are always directed to licensed physicians, specialized medical clinics, or local emergency services for formal healthcare interventions.
